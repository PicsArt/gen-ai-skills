# Templates

These are suggested starting points. The user's brief overrides every default, including count,
length, casting, setting and ending. Count and length are brief decisions; never silently adopt them.
Use one spine across a run of episodes rather than assigning each step exactly one episode.

| Template | Suggested count × length | Emotional promise and beat spine | Opening register | Ending register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Billionaire Romance | 40 × 75 s | A powerful person chooses the protagonist at a real cost. Ordinary life → antagonistic meeting → forced proximity → love → secret → separation → costly gesture → chosen future | Humiliation or forced proximity already happening | A name, photograph or arrival shifts intimacy and leverage |
| Revenge | 40 × 70 s | Those who caused the loss must reckon with it. Loss → disappearance → skilled return → targeted strikes → complication → true enemy → reckoning | The cost or an enemy realizing whom they face | Recognition, exposure or power changing hands |
| Family Secrets | 80 × 75 s | The outsider proves what the family concealed. Contradiction → evidence → conflict → deeper secret → parentage turn → inheritance battle → truth | A document, image or sentence contradicts the family story | Evidence changes the meaning of the previous answer |
| Fantasy Romance | 40 × 80 s | Being seen brings love and danger. Ordinary life → impossible encounter → powers → forbidden bond → ancient secret → conflict → sacrifice | A world rule visibly breaks | A threat becomes closer or more personal |
| Romantic Comedy | 20 × 65 s | A disaster worth enjoying becomes a chosen relationship. Accident → forced together → escalating lie → feelings → misunderstanding → admission | The mess is already in progress | A lie grows, collides with another, or reaches the wrong person |

An ending register is a tone, not a fixed shape. Rotate the six shapes in `serialization.md`.
The finale pays the promise with a settled image. A bittersweet landing keeps the cost real.

## Customize the spine

| Template | Useful choices to extract | Cast and sets | Theme and captions to suggest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Billionaire Romance | Whose competence, which power structure, fake relationship or enemies-to-lovers, chosen future | Proud skilled lead, guarded powerful partner, reputation keeper; office, home, public event | Piano/strings; clean sans, two lines, turn word emphasized |
| Revenge | What was taken, covert or open return, justice or mercy | Skilled returnee, visible enemy, hidden beneficiary, ally; loss site, workplace, public reckoning | Low strings/percussion; condensed sans, accusation clear |
| Family Secrets | Parentage, inheritance or crime; whose lie; what truth costs | Excluded heir, family gatekeeper, outsider with access; family house, archive, business | Piano/cello, childhood motif; readable names and dates |
| Fantasy Romance | World rules, inherited gift, forbidden bond, sacrifice kept or survived | Outsider, dangerous partner, enforcing elder, withholding mentor; ordinary room, lore space, ritual set | Strings and low pulse; readable oaths, restrained emphasis |
| Romantic Comedy | Forced proximity, who lies, when feelings become mutual, what the admission costs | Capable lead with a personal blind spot, straight-faced partner, badly timed rival; home, work, café | Light plucked motif; rounded sans, timing preserved |

Keep casting open to the user's genders, relationships and world. Do not import demographic
claims or force a template's archetype over supplied people. Suggest only missing craft choices.

## Freeform

Reflect the user's premise in one line. Extract characters, setting, language, tone, ending and
constraints already present. Name the nearest spine only if it helps; an invented spine is valid.
Leave missing brief decisions to the brief. Fill proposed craft gaps with labeled inferences, then
resolve the same Story DNA fields as a template. Carry the user's words through unchanged.

## Trending

Use Trending only when the host has web search. If it does not, say that current trends cannot
be checked here and offer a fixed spine or Freeform. Never present remembered trends as current.

Establish audience and tone. Search the current month and year for rising tropes, recent examples
and saturation. Derive three to five concepts with title, logline, cited evidence, nearest spine,
differentiator and suggested count and length. Distinguish evidence from inference. Say when a
spine appears saturated. If results are unusable, say so and return to fixed templates. A picked
concept still needs the user's count, length and full bible lock.
