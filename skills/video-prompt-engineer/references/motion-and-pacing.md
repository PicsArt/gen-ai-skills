# Motion and pacing

Read [motion timing](craft-refinements.md#motion-timing) for speed profiles and [performance](craft-refinements.md#performance) for subject action.

Keep camera travel, subject movement, focus and editing separate. A still subject and a locked camera can be deliberate. Smooth movement need not be slow; constant speed need not include easing. Use explicit intervals for acceleration, steady travel, deceleration and holds. On curved paths, constant speed does not mean an unchanging velocity vector.

Example: “Begin on the wide view. Arc around the stationary object at an even pace, then decelerate into the final three-quarter view and hold.” Relative phases are a plan, not measured timing.

Blur, slowed playback and camera speed are distinct. Do not call a warped silhouette motion blur or promise high-frame-rate capture because a prompt mentions it. Use supported settings when available and inspect the output at playback speed.
