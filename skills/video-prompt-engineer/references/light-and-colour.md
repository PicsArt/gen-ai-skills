# Light and colour

Read [lighting](craft-refinements.md#lighting) to plan sources and [film look](craft-refinements.md#film-look) for finishing.

Direction describes where light comes from; quality describes shadow-edge softness. Intensity and fill are separate. A broad apparent source can make soft edges while leaving deep shadows. Backlighting need not create an outline around everything. Name how much front detail is wanted.

Example: “Broad window light from camera-left, gradual cheek shadows, gentle room bounce preserving the far eye; restrained warm wood, natural skin colour and readable fabric highlights.”

Palette, contrast, grain, bloom and halation should be independently chosen when relevant. Keep a clean version for controlled finishing when matching matters. A film-stock name does not establish exact emulation, and a requested brand hex does not guarantee rendered colour. Inspect the finished export.
