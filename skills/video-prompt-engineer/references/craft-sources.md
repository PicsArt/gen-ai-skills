# Research scope and sources

Integrated from Shotcraft authored research checked 2026-11-20. This is not a fresh source audit or a controlled model benchmark. The 64 camera cases informed vocabulary; public reel advice informed hypotheses about realism, performance and continuity. Technical sources support physical or finishing distinctions only. All prompt examples are original and untested. No footage, transcripts, private courses or comment-to-DM material is redistributed. Product presentation guidance is editorial and does not establish quality or value.

- [Motion blur and slow motion: AI Video Bootcamp](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1430838362491034): Creator explanation reviewed through automatic transcription and sampled frames. Camera language is not proof of a generator control. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Cameras](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/cameras.html): Documented virtual-camera behavior; does not establish that a generative model simulates those controls. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Motion blur](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/cycles/render_settings/motion_blur.html): Renderer shutter behavior, not generative-model capability evidence. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [AI Video Bootcamp: lighting examples](https://www.facebook.com/reel/4519958271585801): Public caption, automatic transcript and chronological sampled frames reviewed; not a controlled model test. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [ARRI Lighting Handbook, fourth edition](https://www.arri.com/resource/blob/83996/409091c612f371b0c68b41d9dcb636db/arri-lighting-handbook-english-data.pdf): Physical lighting principles. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [AI Video Bootcamp: film-stock vocabulary](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1448932727114917): Public caption, automatic transcript and chronological sampled frames reviewed; not a controlled model test. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [DaVinci Resolve 19 New Features Guide, Film Look Creator](https://documents.blackmagicdesign.com/SupportNotes/DaVinci_Resolve_19_New_Features_Guide.pdf): Documented finishing controls, not proof of generative-model behavior. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Four realism cues: AI Video Bootcamp](https://www.facebook.com/reel/2331023024404277): Creator advice from the public caption and automatic transcript. No controlled prompt comparison or success rate supplied. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Surface reflection](https://www.pbr-book.org/3ed-2018/Color_and_Radiometry/Surface_Reflection): Physical rendering reference, not a test of video prompts. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Conversation geography: AI Video Bootcamp](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1445237557548604): Creator advice reviewed through automatic transcription. Its absolute rule against lens contact is not adopted here. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Fitchburg State: The Shot List](https://openbooks.fitchburgstate.edu/preproduction/chapter/the-shot-list/): Shot-size abbreviations EWS, WS, MWS, MS, MCU, CU, ECU and OS; shot-list elements. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Yale Film Analysis: Cinematography](https://filmanalysis.yale.edu/cinematography/): Pan, tilt, framing scales, rack focus and the medium-long / American-shot terminology. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Shaner and Jones: Shots and Shot Plans](https://ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com/images/chap05_0321238338/elementLinks/chap05_0321238338.pdf): Published textbook chapter: singles, dirty singles, inserts, masters and shot scale; pp. 116–127. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Columbia: Tracking (Trucking) Shot](https://filmglossary.ccnmtl.columbia.edu/term/tracking-trucking-shot/): Tracking is broader than a lateral move or following from behind. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Columbia: Camera Angle](https://filmglossary.ccnmtl.columbia.edu/term/camera-angle/): Eye-level, high-angle and low-angle terminology. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Columbia: Canted Angle (Dutch Angle)](https://filmglossary.ccnmtl.columbia.edu/term/canted-angle-dutch-angle/): Canted angle and Dutch angle are alternative names. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [City College of San Francisco: Camera Movements](https://human.libretexts.org/Courses/City_College_of_San_Francisco/Video_Production_Handbook_I/08:_Video_Aesthetics/8.03:_Camera_Movements): Basic movement vocabulary, including push in / pull out and lateral trucking. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [In Depth Cine: Crash Zoom](https://www.indepthcine.com/videos/crash-zoom): Practitioner explanation of rapid optical zooming in either direction. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Adobe: Cowboy Shot](https://www.adobe.com/au/creativecloud/video/production/cinematography/camera-shots-and-angles/cowboy-shot.html): Cinematographer commentary and the cowboy / American-shot names. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [American Cinematographer: One Shot With Ed Sheeran](https://theasc.com/article/one-shot-ed-sheeran-cinematography/): Cinematographer Nyk Allen uses oner and distinguishes a true continuous take from hidden joins. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Snorricam: SC Pro Standard](https://www.snorricam.com/Products/SC-Pro-Standard): Manufacturer documentation for the named performer-mounted camera system. Research check: 2026-11-20.
- [Tiffen: Steadicam Volt 3](https://tiffen.com/pages/steadicam-volt-3): Steadicam is a named stabilization system; models can include electronic assistance. Research check: 2026-11-20.
