# Constraints and exclusions

Include only constraints relevant to the brief. Preserve requested logos, text, people, movement and sound. A mandatory generic sentence beginning with “No” is not portable across models.

Use a documented negative field where the endpoint supports one; otherwise follow its guidance and prefer a positive description of the desired behavior. Do not repeat the same constraints in both fields. Unknown support stays unknown.

Examples: “camera locked off”; “the approved product retains its exact silhouette and matte finish”; “the room remains empty apart from the named subject.” Do not replace shallow depth of field with “tack sharp throughout” merely to avoid accidental blur.

An exclusion is not a guarantee against anatomy errors, drift, text or watermarks. Review the result. Do not remove or claim control over provenance markers supplied by the platform. Exact typography or branding may need approved assets and compositing, as a separate task.
