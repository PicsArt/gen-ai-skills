# Worked examples

These are original, untested prompt edits. Preserve all unrelated clauses in a real prompt.

## Camera-only repair

Request: “Fix camera only: the adult maker holds the approved cup in a workshop. Warm window light, silent, vertical. Camera pans closer to the rim.”

Revised camera clause: “Push in toward the cup, keeping its rim in frame as it fills the final close-up.”

Change note: Replaced rotation with forward travel to express the requested approach. The subject, light, sound and format stay unchanged.

## Timing conflict

Request: “Constant speed for the entire shot, with a gradual stop before it ends.”

Clarify: “Should the move remain constant-speed until the clip cuts, or travel at constant speed through the middle and then ease to a stop?” Both explicit requirements cannot hold over the same interval.

## Defect report

Request: “There is an extra hand,” with a prompt but no clip attached.

Inspect the intended people, grip, contact and reference coverage. Revise ambiguous action within scope and suggest inspecting the difficult frames. Do not infer a proven cause from the complaint or claim that a missing exclusion caused the defect. Do not remove a participant or crop out a required demonstration without making that an explicit alternative.

## Skin-only repair

Original clause: “Perfectly smooth skin on the approved older woman as she turns.”

Replacement: “Preserve the approved woman's age, skin tone, makeup and distinctive marks through the turn, with subtle visible skin texture and restrained highlights.”

Review the whole turn for identity and attached texture; do not manufacture freckles or change her age.
