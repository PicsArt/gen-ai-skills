# Camera

Separate framing, camera height/aim, travel, focus and support. Read the [camera refinement](craft-refinements.md#camera) for distinctions and professional aliases.

- Push in / dolly in: move toward a subject. Pull back / dolly out: move away.
- Pan / tilt: rotate from a fixed position. Truck left/right: move sideways.
- Boom up/down: move vertically; state any simultaneous tilt separately.
- Zoom: change focal length from the same viewpoint. Crash/snap/whip zoom describes a rapid variant.
- Rack focus: shift the sharp plane between named targets, with possible breathing. It is not camera travel.
- Locked off: fixed camera. Stabilized tracking, handheld, aerial and performer-mounted require a path/behavior if motion matters.

Expand shot-list abbreviations: WS wide shot, MS medium shot, MCU medium close-up, CU close-up, ECU extreme close-up, OTS over-the-shoulder, POV point of view. No one crop boundary is universal across crews.

Camera distance governs perspective; field of view also depends on format and focal length. Do not present 50mm as universally human vision or 85mm as guaranteed flattering. Describe the intended framing, spatial relationship and sharp subject. A generator may not physically simulate lens numbers.

Example: “Push in toward the cup, keeping its rim in frame; the foreground cloth moves out of view as the cup fills the final close-up.” This is an untested creative instruction.
