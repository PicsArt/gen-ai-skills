# Video Prompt: Product Detail

## Scope and output

Use the supplied prompt and requested change. If no prompt is supplied, ask for it; do not invent a new brief. No generation tools, paid jobs or publication are needed.

Keep subject, action, identity, brand requirements, duration, aspect ratio, audio and references unchanged unless the request explicitly changes them. Replace conflicting in-scope wording rather than appending contradictions. If two explicit requirements conflict and the intended outcome is unclear, ask one focused question; otherwise make the smallest useful edit and label assumptions. Do not require a model choice for a model-neutral wording repair.

Return the revised full prompt in a copyable block (or only the replacement clause if requested), a short change note, assumptions only when needed, and one relevant output check. Do not claim the edit is tested or will cure a model defect. When a model is named, use its documented controls and limits; unknown capabilities remain unknown.

## Refinement decisions

Keep the approved product’s silhouette, dimensions, finish, colour, markings, wear and working state. Distinguish presentation from condition: a used component can be photographed carefully without becoming new or hiding a defect. Do not invent performance, testimonials or a functioning mechanism from visual polish.

Choose a useful detail and describe how composition/light reveal it: machining, seams, glaze, fabric or glass. Specify a clear contact/support surface, coherent reflections and a restrained background. Premium presentation is a creative treatment, not proof of quality or value. Translate luxury into observable material, light, framing and pacing rather than a living creator or fashion-house imitation.

If a presenter is requested, preserve the chosen adult identity, wardrobe, task and expertise; do not assign expertise or product category by gender or attractiveness. For demonstrations, maintain grip, contact and the real mechanism. Keep exact logos/labels from approved assets or place them in post; do not promise generated typography accuracy.

Keep brand palette and approved references. Do not turn a camera-only request into a new luxury concept, swap a model, or polish a matte/aged surface without permission. Where no reference supports fine detail, call it unknown rather than inventing it.

## Example

Original: Make this used matte metal part look expensive as it rotates on the table.

Revision: Present the approved used matte metal part with a restrained background and a clean side highlight that reveals the machining. Preserve the matte finish, visible wear and exact silhouette as it rotates on the table.

This illustrates a scoped edit, not a tested model recipe. Keep any other clauses in the actual user's prompt.

## Review

Check silhouette, wear, finish, labels, reflections, contact and any claimed function throughout the take.
