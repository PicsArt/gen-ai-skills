# Thumbnail

Make a video thumbnail or cover that states the topic clearly and stays readable when small.

## When to Use

- Create a thumbnail or cover for a video from its content or brief.
- Keep the headline and subject readable at the destination's small display size.

## Prerequisites

- The `picsart` MCP server is connected.
- Read [the interface map](../picsart-workflows/references/interface-map.md) before tool use.

## How to Run

1. Define the thumbnail's factual message from the video or supplied brief.
2. Record the destination, crop, required subject, and exact headline, when supplied.
3. Check current destination specifications before export.

## Quick Reference

- Use [generation](../picsart-generate/SKILL.md) for required imagery.
- Use [scene design](../picsart-scene-design/SKILL.md) for exact text and logo placement.

## Procedure

1. Choose the visual detail that best communicates the message.
2. Give that detail a clear position and contrast against its background.
3. Remove competing details unless they explain the topic.
4. Use supplied person or product references when identity matters.
5. Treat a style reference as evidence of design choices, without reproducing its content or arrangement.
6. Keep variants within the requested count and budget.
7. Label the visual choice that each variant changes.
8. Inspect the image at full size and at the destination's small display size.
9. Check subject recognition, headline legibility, crop, reference fidelity, and factual meaning.
10. Repair the failed element with [image edit](../picsart-image-edit/SKILL.md) or a scene change.

## Pitfalls

- Do not imply an event or result absent from the source content.
- Do not promise click performance without measured results.

## Verification

- Deliver the requested covers with clear variant labels.
- Use [delivery check](../picsart-delivery-check/SKILL.md) for final image files.
