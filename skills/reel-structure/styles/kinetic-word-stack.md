The stack keeps one band for the whole reel, the one every kept photo leaves free of faces: the top band (y 330, 480, 630, 780, 930) or the bottom band (y 760, 910, 1060, 1210, 1360). Before building, look at every photo: drop any photo whose face (with 60 px around it) enters the chosen band, and if neither band is free on at least 4 photos, the style doesn't fit. Band: every word inside y 250–1440 and x 65–1015; readable: a word may never touch a face |
