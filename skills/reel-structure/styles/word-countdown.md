- ID: word-countdown
- Format: a five-to-one countdown in words over photos
- Length: 8.4 s
- Cut rate: ~50 cuts/min while counting (one shot every 1.2 s)
- Caption style: one big serif number word per shot, a small line under it
- Bed: steady beat with a hit every 1.2 s, ~100 BPM
- Layout: single
- Source: our style library
