# Research basis and boundaries

Reviewed 25 October 2026. This is a reusable evidence summary, not a claim that these examples are current winning formats. Refresh platform/model details and trend examples for live work.

## Useful evidence

- [George Loewenstein, The Psychology of Curiosity (1994)](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/sds/docs/loewenstein/PsychofCuriosity.pdf): specific information gaps are a useful explanation of curiosity. Application to Reel structure is an inference; the paper does not establish an optimal hook duration or guarantee viewing.
- [Berger and Milkman, What Makes Online Content Viral? (2012)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1509/jmr.10.0353): emotion, activation and useful/interesting material relate to sharing in the studied contexts. It is not a trial of Instagram commerce hooks.
- [Jonah Berger, Viral's Secret Formula](https://jonahberger.com/virals-secret-formula/): social currency and STEPPS offer a useful sharing lens; Berger explicitly rejects a guaranteed audience size.
- [Wharton, Catching Eyes in the Attention Economy (2024)](https://magazine.wharton.upenn.edu/issues/spring-summer-2024/catching-eyes-in-the-attention-economy/): discusses linguistic attention and processing ease. Applying clarity to audiovisual scripts is a design inference, not an Instagram experiment.
- [Meta, Trial Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/): non-follower-first testing and comparative engagement feedback. This is not randomized audience allocation.
- [Meta, Inspiring Creativity (2025)](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/inspiring-creativity-that-brings-people-together/): broader Trial Reels availability and observational creator results; not a promise of growth.
- [TikTok Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/creative-codes): hook/body/close, vertical composition, UI awareness, sound and stimulation. Paid TikTok creative guidance transfers as a test hypothesis, not a universal organic Instagram rule.
- [Tamilore Oladipo, Buffer, Good Hooks](https://buffer.com/resources/good-hooks/): multi-element openings and promise/payoff alignment. Useful practitioner synthesis; its broad algorithm and demographic statements need separate verification.

## Model and skill inputs

[ByteDance Seedance 2.5 announcement](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5) and live Picsart catalog/parameter reads confirmed the requested model. Use the provider's current schema when executing; support on one provider does not establish support on another.

Installed film-development, film-scenes and PROMPT-BLOCKS guidance contributed complete shot blocks, explicit references, coherent geography and timing. Instagram-brand-content contributed factual catalog checks, single-goal CTAs and brand-specific media rules. These are workflow practices, not evidence of conversion lift. The standalone audit accompanying this skill documents scope and depth.

## Claims explicitly rejected

No guarantee of views, shares or sales; no claim that viewers are forced to finish an open loop; no universal two-second attention reset; no requirement to loop; no universal sound-on percentage; no assumption that absurdity is cognitive dissonance; no fabricated proof or false urgency; no claim that 5,000 template combinations equal 5,000 good ideas. Generated VFX cannot substantiate physical product performance.
