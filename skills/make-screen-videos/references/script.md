# The presenter's script

Only for a promo with a presenter. The lines are written for the presenter's mouth, not for a
reader, and they follow the spec: one line per step the spec keeps, in the same order.

| Part | Length spoken | Shown as | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | the first 2 s (≤ 8 words) | the presenter on camera, full frame, over the built hook | The hook rule in `spec.md`: a problem or a result, never a greeting. "Stop resizing every post by hand." not "Hi, today I'll show you…" |
| **Steps** | each within its step's length in the build's `timeline` | a head bubble over the panel, or no presenter | One line per step, present tense, the outcome not the clicks. Name UI only when the viewer must find it |
| **Outro** | ≤ 3 s | the results grid with the call to action, or the presenter | One call to action, the same as the spec's |

The spoken line is not the words on screen. The screen already carries the request or the
feature line, so the voice adds what the screen cannot: why it matters, or what the viewer saves.
Read aloud, the same words twice sound like a reading.

The spoken lines carry the loops as the words on screen do (`spec.md`, Loops).

Spoken lines follow the four rules in `spec.md`, The words (one anchor, a number when proved, show
don't claim, but and so), and four of their own:

- **Say "you".** "You get the whole deck back" not "Users can generate decks".
- **Short words, one idea per line.** A line the presenter says in one breath, with the stress on
  its last word.
- **A guide, never a customer.** The presenter shows the product; they never say they bought it,
  use it or were helped by it. A generated face vouching for a product is a fake testimonial.
- **No direction in the words.** How a line is said is the voice-over-generation skill's.

Size each line to its step as the voice-over-generation skill says. Review the lines as quality-review's script
review says before they are quoted, so no voice is paid for a script that should not be made.
