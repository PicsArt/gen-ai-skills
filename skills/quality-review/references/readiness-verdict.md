# Readiness verdict

Use after collecting review evidence and before delivery. This consumes existing evidence; it
does not authorize extra inspection, generation or spend. The checks themselves are the rest of this skill.

Before generation, record one to three decisive requirements with their expected interval,
visible before/change/after, failure condition and inspection method. Include a prohibited event
when its absence matters. Fix unreadable required artwork in the staging before paying for motion.

After generation, pair a whole-video audience read with focused coverage of those requirements.
The audience read describes what happened without a script supplying the missing meaning. It tests
comprehension. For decisive action, use supported measured excerpts or original-resolution frames
with adequate temporal coverage. Rephrasing a whole-video question is not an independent focused
check. A frame may prove a visible defect; it cannot prove continuous absence. For lettering,
request a literal transcription or unreadable without giving the expected word. If evidence
conflicts, make at most one focused independent check within the recorded allowance, then keep
any unresolved conflict. Do not take a majority vote or erase adverse observations.

For each binding criterion keep `required event | actual coverage | observed fact | pass/fail/unknown`.
A missing interval is an unknown. A task's success proves execution; dimensions prove dimensions;
a transcript supports recognized words; sampled frames show sampled moments. None alone proves
continuous physical action, story coherence, exact dialogue or selected-voice identity.

For a decisive interaction, cover the entire approach/pickup, contact, release and aftermath.
If review begins with an object already held, pickup remains unverified. A failed extraction stays
a failed extraction; a second broad opinion cannot replace missing coverage. Preserve attempt
limits and actual blockers.

| Evidence | Headline |
|---|---|
| Any material observed failure | Needs revision |
| No material failure, but required evidence is missing or disputed | Not yet verified |
| Every material criterion is supported | Reviewed candidate |

Choose exactly one headline. Use it consistently in the checkpoint and reply. User acceptance is
separate. A playable saved candidate proves availability, not readiness. Permission to continue
does not erase a required check. Do not combine Reviewed candidate with a material unknown.

A physical failure in the decisive event outweighs an attractive image. A script is not evidence
that its generated event happened.

A repair retains all requirements. Check dependencies affected by the replacement; a full-frame
edit may change identity, performance and sound beyond the requested fix. Do not reset attempts,
silently remove a required action or extrapolate a failed pilot to the rest of a series.

Compose the reply from `verdict | grouped material issues | next action or concrete blocker`.
Use up to three short sentences and the actual media link. Put the detailed evidence in the
record. For unresolved candidates, avoid a list of passed specifications. Distinguish quoted
costs from known charges. Continue an already authorized next action; otherwise state its blocker.
Never describe an unaccepted candidate as accepted.
